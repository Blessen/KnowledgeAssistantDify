# CUSTOMISED POWER SOLUTIONS FOR DYNAMIC NEEDS
Businesses worldwide face fluctuating power demands. Whether due to daily variations or specific usage patterns, a flexible power solution is essential.

Kirloskar offers the Optiprime, a customized solution ideal for applications with variable power needs. This innovative system tackles fluctuating loads, reducing fuel consumption and CO2 emissions by up to 40%.

The Optiprime's centralized control panel with an integrated Power Management System ensures optimal performance. This intelligent system seamlessly switches on or off the two power packs based on real-time load requirements.

The Optiprime's versatility extends to its configuration options. It can be deployed as a single unit, or two separate units can be combined, with prime and standby power functionalities housed within a single enclosure.
